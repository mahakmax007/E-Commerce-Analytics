# 🛒 E-Commerce Analytics — Customer, Sales & RFM Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes **Brazilian e-commerce data** to uncover insights into sales performance, customer behavior, delivery performance, payment methods, seller performance, and customer segmentation.

The analysis combines **Python, SQL, and Tableau** to transform raw e-commerce data into meaningful business insights and interactive dashboards.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and revenue performance
* Understand customer purchasing behavior
* Identify high-value and at-risk customers
* Perform **RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis**
* Analyze customer segments and their contribution to revenue
* Evaluate seller performance
* Analyze payment methods and order values
* Measure delivery time and freight costs
* Build interactive Tableau dashboards for business reporting

---

## 📊 Dataset

The project uses the **Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist**, containing approximately 100K orders and information about:

* Customers
* Orders
* Order Items
* Products
* Payments
* Reviews
* Sellers
* Geolocation
* Product categories

## 🔍 Data Analysis

The project includes exploratory data analysis covering:

### Sales Analysis

* Total revenue
* Average Order Value (AOV)
* Product price analysis
* Freight cost analysis
* Revenue contribution

### Customer Analysis

* Total customers
* Customer purchasing behavior
* Customer segmentation
* Customer lifetime behavior

### RFM Analysis

Customers were segmented using:

* **Recency** — How recently a customer purchased
* **Frequency** — How frequently a customer purchased
* **Monetary** — How much a customer spent

The analysis identifies customer groups such as:

* Champions
* At Risk
* Lost Customers
* Others

### Seller Analysis

* Seller revenue
* Top-performing sellers
* Seller contribution to total revenue

### Delivery Analysis

### Payment Analysis
